How the figure is computed / كيف يُحسب الرقم
  • Investment = priced quote (subtotal {{ '{:,.2f}'.format(roi.subtotal) }}, discount {{ '{:,.2f}'.format(roi.discount_amount) }}, tax {{ '{:,.2f}'.format(roi.tax_amount) }}).
  • Yearly net = (trees × yield × price) − (trees × operating cost + maintenance + other costs).
  • ROI = ({{ roi.horizon }} × yearly net − investment) ÷ investment × 100.
  • Payback = investment ÷ yearly net; it is open ended and may fall after the {{ roi.horizon }}-year horizon shown here.
