  • Master valve on the main pipe (MP) at the water source.
  • Sector valve where each sector's second pipe (SP) leaves the main pipe.
  • Zone valve where each zone pipe (ZP) leaves its feeding pipe.
  • Each valve takes the diameter of the pipe it switches, so a 90 mm main pipe with a 63 mm second pipe gives a 90 → 63 valve at that point.
